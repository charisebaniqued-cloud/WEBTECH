Why Styling Matters in Css

Good web design starts with making content effortless to read. Choosing a clean sans-serif font, setting an appropriate text size, and adding comfortable line spacing prevent eye strain and keep visitors engaged with your page.

Color contrast is equally important when styling text and backgrounds. Dark text set against a soft, light background creates a balanced visual hierarchy that guides the reader naturally from headings to body copy.

Key Principle of a Consistent Color Scheme

Limiting your palette to three or four primary colors keeps a webpage looking cohesive and intentional rather than cluttered. Each color should serve a clear purpose, such as distinguishing headings, body text, and interactive elements.

  • Use a soft off-white or light gray background to reduce harsh screen glare.
  • Apply dark blue and dark green tones to separate main titles from section headings.
  • Reserve a distinct accent color for links so users immediately recognize them as clickable.
